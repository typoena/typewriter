## Power switch + power path — decided 2026-07-26

A latching panel switch (the case already has the hole, next to the µSD slot on
PCB2 — the case gets updated to match the chosen part, not the reverse) plus a
**power-path (load-sharing)** circuit. The behavior contract, in the three
switch × mains states:

1. **Switch off, on mains** → device off, battery charges (unloaded, terminates
   cleanly). Standby drain ≈ DW01 protection only (µA).
2. **Switch on, on mains** → device runs **from mains**; the battery charges
   untouched — zero cycles spent while docked.
3. **Switch on, mains pulled** → instant (µs, body-diode) switchover to
   battery; no brownout, nothing to detect in firmware.

The HW-373 alone can't do this: its OUT pins feed the load *through the battery
path*, so a docked, running device would micro-cycle the cell and the TP4056
would never terminate. Three extra parts fix it:

```
USB-C mains ──5V──┬────────────► TP4056 (HW-373) ──B+──► [JST-PH] LiPo 3700 mAh
                  │                                          │
                  │ SS34 Schottky                            │ (OUT+, via DW01 FETs)
                  ▼                                          ▼
                  ├◄──────────── AO3401 P-FET ◄──────────────┘
                  │              gate → 5V mains, 100k pulldown
                  ▼
            [load node] ──► LATCHING SWITCH ──► MT3608 ──► 5V rail
```

Mains present holds the P-FET gate high (off) and feeds the load through the
Schottky (~4.7 V into the boost — fine, it only ever steps up); mains gone, the
body diode conducts immediately, then the gate falls and the FET carries the
battery current at low loss.

**Placement rules (invariants):**

- The switch cuts the **load path only** (load node → MT3608 input). Never put
  it between battery and charger, or state 1 stops charging.
- Battery is hard-wired to B+/B− — its charge path has no switch, no diode.
- Size the switch for ~2 A: MT3608 input peaks near that at 3.7 V in during an
  e-paper refresh + Wi-Fi burst.

**Hazards:**

- ⚠️ JST **polarity is not standardized** across battery vendors — verify red
  lands on B+ against the PCB2 footprint before first plug-in; a reversed pack
  kills the charger board.
- The latching button needs **~20–25 mm of body depth** behind the panel plus
  wire clearance — check against the PCB2/case pocket before freezing the case
  update. Hole is Ø12 mm.
- An LED-ring button would bleed the battery whenever on; the picked variant
  has none. A charging indicator, if wanted, belongs on TP4056 `CHRG`/`STDBY`
  (mains-powered), not on the button.
