# Manufacturing — Typoena enclosure

The model is nominal geometry. These two corrections are applied at slicing.
Without them the parts do not assemble.

| correction | value |
| --- | --- |
| **Scale** | **X = Y = 101.37 %**, Z = 100 % |
| **XY compensation** | **0.08 mm per side** — contours shrink, holes grow |

Measured: 1.35 % shrink, 0.164 mm over-extrusion across a feature (hence 0.08 a
side). Z needs no correction.
